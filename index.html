About Casa Fina

Rosie’s is an intimate, lively wine bar located off Blanco Street behind Howards Bar & Club. Our eclectic rotating wine list features bottles from around the world with an emphasis on natural winemaking from unique terroirs and small producers. Our menu features small plates inspired by the cuisines of Spain, Portugal, and France. The cozy space features a long bar and secluded patio. Rosie’s is walk-in friendly and does not take reservations.
