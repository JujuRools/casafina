About Casa Fina

Casa Fina is a small, lively Spanish restaurant at 408 Josephine Street in Austin. Inspired by the warmth of European dining culture, it’s a place to settle in, share a meal, and let the conversation linger. Our menu celebrates the flavors of Spain in a cozy setting that feels familiar from the moment you arrive. Whether you’re gathering with family, catching up with friends, or making new ones around the table, we hope you’ll feel right at home.

The Chef

Raised in Italy, chef Erind Halilaj brings a deep appreciation for good food and the experience of sharing it. His cooking is rooted in respect for tradition, fresh ingredients, and classic techniques—a philosophy that puts care into every plate and hospitality at the heart of the meal.
